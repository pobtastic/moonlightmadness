E666: Play Tune
Used by the routines at Handler_Interrupts and Animate_Introduction.
Plays one note from Data_TuneStream / Data_TuneStreamCopy through the speaker. BC is the note length (1106 in-game, 0A14 on the introduction).
Play_Tune E666 LD A,($647C) Jump to JumpTable_Delay if *SkipDelayFlag is set.

E66A JP NZ,JumpTable_Delay
E66D LD HL,($6469) Read the next Data_TuneStream byte, wrapping at FF.

E671 CP $FF
E673 JR NZ,Play_Tune_StorePitch
E675 LD HL,$B491
E678 LD ($646B),HL
E67B LD HL,$B370
E67E LD A,(HL)
Play_Tune_StorePitch E67F INC HL Store the pitch and read the matching Data_TuneStreamCopy duration.

E698 JP NZ,Play_Tune_Tone
